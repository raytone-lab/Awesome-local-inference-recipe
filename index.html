What the source levels mean

Official

Numbers published by the project or vendor, usually best-case.

Measured

The author ran it on their own machine, with a screenshot or log.

Relayed

A second-hand quote of someone else’s result; check the original post.

Claimed

A prediction or no reproduction details; for reference only.
